## 1. Purpose

This Agreement sets out the terms under which the Photographer provides photographic and immersive 360-degree imaging services for properties located primarily in the Tangier-Tetouan region of Morocco.

The services may be performed for real estate agencies, developers, property owners or other clients using the Platform, each referred to as a **“Client.”**

This Agreement is intended to:

- define the Photographer’s service obligations;
- protect the Platform’s commercial and personal information;
- prevent the circumvention of the Platform; and
- establish the financial consequences of a breach of this Agreement.

---

## 2. Independent Contractor Status

The Photographer acts as an independent service provider. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, relationship of subordination, partnership, joint venture, general agency, commercial agency or general exclusivity between the Parties.

The Photographer may not bind the Platform or represent the Platform before any third party without the Platform’s prior written authorization.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Photographer is responsible for their own equipment, transportation, insurance, tax filings and administrative obligations.

---

## 3. Services and Service Levels

For each assignment, the Platform may provide the Photographer with the information necessary to perform the services, including:

- the property address or location;
- the appointment date and time;
- the Client’s relevant contact details;
- the technical requirements for the assignment; and
- the delivery deadline for the files.

The Photographer agrees to:

1. accept or decline each assignment within the period specified by the Platform;
2. arrive on time with suitable and functional equipment;
3. perform the photography and 360-degree imaging services in accordance with the Platform’s technical instructions;
4. comply with all applicable safety, access and confidentiality requirements at the property;
5. deliver the files in the required format and within the specified deadline;
6. immediately notify the Platform of any delay, inability to attend, incident or difficulty;
7. refrain from modifying, deleting, disclosing or using Client data for any purpose other than performing the assignment.

Unless otherwise stated in the assignment, any technical defect attributable to the Photographer must be corrected within a reasonable period and, where necessary, without additional charge.

---

## 4. Access to Confidential Information

In connection with the services, the Photographer may access confidential information, including:

- property addresses and locations;
- names, telephone numbers and email addresses of Clients;
- information concerning real estate agencies and developers;
- sale prices, rental prices and transaction terms;
- appointment schedules and access information;
- files, images, plans, login details and information available through the Platform; and
- the Platform’s commercial, technical or strategic information.

All such information is referred to as **“Confidential Information.”**

Confidential Information does not include information that:

- was publicly available before it was disclosed;
- becomes publicly available without a breach of this Agreement;
- was lawfully known to the Photographer before disclosure; or
- must be disclosed under applicable law or a decision of a competent authority, provided that the Photographer gives the Platform prior notice where legally permitted.

---

## 5. Confidentiality and Personal Data Protection

The Photographer agrees to:

- use Confidential Information solely to perform assignments authorized by the Platform;
- refrain from copying, selling, transferring, publishing or exploiting Confidential Information for personal purposes;
- retain data only for as long as necessary to perform the relevant assignment;
- refrain from transferring data to any third party without the Platform’s prior authorization;
- take reasonable measures to protect data against loss, unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse;
- secure their telephone, computer, camera equipment, storage systems and user accounts; and
- delete or return data and documents upon the Platform’s request.

The Photographer must not use addresses, telephone numbers, transaction prices or other information obtained through the Platform to solicit a Client, conduct a transaction with the Client or offer competing services.

Personal data shall be processed in accordance with **Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data**, together with its implementing provisions and other applicable Moroccan laws.

The Photographer shall immediately notify the Platform of any security incident, data loss, unauthorized access or accidental disclosure, and in any event no later than **twenty-four (24) hours** after becoming aware of it.

The Photographer shall not process personal data for any purpose other than the purpose set out in this Agreement and the relevant assignment.

---

## 6. Non-Circumvention

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of **twenty-four (24) months from the date on which the Photographer accessed, directly or indirectly, information relating to a Client or assignment**, the Photographer shall not, directly or indirectly, for their own account or for the account of any third party:

1. contact a Client introduced or identified by the Platform, except to the extent strictly necessary to perform an authorized assignment;
2. offer or provide that Client with photography, virtual-tour, imaging, consulting or other services outside the Platform;
3. enter into a contract or transaction with that Client for the purpose of bypassing the Platform;
4. accept a direct request from the Client for an assignment obtained through information or contact supplied by the Platform;
5. encourage a Client to stop using the Platform or to deal directly with the Photographer; or
6. use contact details or other information obtained through the Platform to solicit the Client or the Client’s business partners.

Any communication necessary to perform an assignment should, whenever possible, take place through the Platform or be reported to the Platform.

This restriction does not apply if the Photographer proves that they had a genuine, independent and pre-existing professional relationship with the Client before accessing the Platform’s data and can provide written evidence of that relationship.

The non-circumvention obligation applies only to Clients and contacts to whom the Photographer was introduced, whose contact details were received, or about whom information was obtained through the Platform.

- a breach of this Agreement;
- a serious or repeated failure to meet the required service levels;
